# Privacy Policy for ScreenNote — Record & Annotate

**Effective Date:** June 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **ScreenNote — Record & Annotate**. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension handles your data. The core philosophy of ScreenNote is that your data belongs to you. Therefore, our extension operates entirely locally on your device and does not transmit any personal information to external servers.

## 2. Information We Access and Why
To provide its advertised "Record & Annotate" functionality, the extension requires access to certain local data and browser capabilities:

- **Website Content:** When you choose to annotate or record a webpage, the extension accesses the visual content of the currently active tab. This is strictly required to allow you to draw over the webpage, capture screenshots, and record the screen.
- **User Activity (Mouse Movements & Clicks):** The extension tracks your cursor movements and clicks solely when the annotation mode is active. This data is used exclusively to translate your actions into drawings, highlights, and text on the screen.
- **Local Storage:** We use your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) to save your preferences, such as whether the annotation mode is toggled on, your selected drawing tool, and your privacy settings. This ensures a seamless experience when switching tabs or reopening the browser.

## 3. How We Handle Your Data (No Data Collection)
- **Local Processing:** All annotations, screenshots, and video recordings generated by the extension are processed and saved entirely on your local hard drive. 
- **No Remote Servers:** We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or store any personal data, web history, screen recordings, or user activity on external servers.
- **No Third-Party Sharing:** Because we do not collect any user data off your device, we do not (and cannot) sell, trade, or transfer any information to third parties.

## 4. Required Chrome Permissions Explained
- `activeTab`: Used to inject the drawing tools and canvas onto the specific webpage you are currently viewing only when you click the extension icon.
- `scripting`: Used to programmatically load the extension's user interface and drawing scripts into your active tab.
- `storage`: Used to save your local tool preferences and extension state.
- `tabCapture`: Used to capture the video and audio of your active tab when you explicitly start a screen recording session.

## 5. Third-Party Services
The extension does not integrate with any external third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services. All code runs independently within your browser.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the extension does not collect personal contact information, we encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes. 

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please feel free to open an issue in this repository.
